## 🔍 What is Preuve?

Preuve is an **AI market research and competitive intelligence platform for startup ideas**. It runs an idea through 60+ live sources (Crunchbase, Reddit, G2, Google Trends) to map demand, competitors, sentiment, and risks, then scores viability from 0 to 100, every finding linked to a source you can verify. As of September 2026, 8,500+ ideas analyzed. In our July 2026 benchmark, only 17.5% scored 70 or above, the launch-ready line.

Real signals, not a confident AI score with nothing behind it. Founders use it to pressure-test ideas before building. Agencies, accelerators, and consultants use it white-label to pre-qualify inbound startups. *(Preuve is French for "proof".)*

Paste one sentence describing your idea. 60 seconds later, free, no card:

- ✅ A **0-to-100 viability score** with a clear go / no-go verdict
- 🔗 **Every claim source-linked**: click through to the Reddit thread, the funding round, the G2 review
- 🏢 **Real competitors** with pricing, funding, and weak spots
- 📈 **Demand signals** from Google Trends, Reddit, Quora, and Product Hunt
- ⚠️ **The risk that breaks the idea** before you build it

## ⚙️ How does Preuve work?

Preuve is a network of 10 specialized research agents that scan 60+ live sources (Crunchbase, Reddit, G2, Product Hunt, Google Trends) in parallel:

| Agent | Role |
|---|---|
| 📊 **Market Analyst** | Sizes the market from real data (TAM / SAM / SOM) |
| 🕵️ **Competitor Scout** | Finds real competitors with pricing, funding, weak spots |
| 🗣️ **Community Researcher** | Scans Reddit and Quora for people describing the pain |
| 💰 **Financial Analyst** | Stress-tests unit economics and pricing |
| 📰 **News Scanner** | Pulls recent funding rounds, launches, and shutdowns |
| ✅ **Cross-Validator** | Fact-checks every claim the other agents make |

When scoring runs disagree, Preuve reruns the analysis until the verdict is stable. Paid reports run on Claude Opus 5.5, Anthropic's most capable model.

## 🛡️ Limitations & Honest Caveats

- Preuve validates **demand and competition**, not your execution. A 90-score idea can still fail in weak hands
- Sources are live web data; a very new niche with no public footprint can score lower than it deserves
- The free scan is a teaser by design: full market sizing, the GTM plan, and pivots are in the $59 report
- The score is a model assessment of the evidence, not a probability of success. No tool can promise that
- Preuve reports what the data shows, not what you hope to hear. Most ideas score below the launch-ready line

## ❓ FAQ

**Is Preuve free?**
In most countries, yes. The Reality Check is free with no credit card. In about 60 seconds it returns a viability score with a verdict, a market size read, 2 competitor previews, and the main blockers. In South Asia and most of Africa, scans start with Starter Pro, a one-time regional offer.

**How accurate is Preuve?**
Every sourced claim links to its source, so you can verify each one yourself, and estimates are labeled as estimates. When scoring runs disagree, Preuve reruns the analysis until the verdict is stable. A paid report cites a median of 70 source links.

**Is Preuve better than validating with ChatGPT?**
ChatGPT answers from model memory and tends to flatter. Preuve pulls live data from 60+ sources at scan time, links every claim, and only 17.5% of ideas clear the 70+ launch-ready line.
